Frequently Asked Questions

How long does CCTV installation take?
Installation timelines vary based on property size and the complexity of the system. A standard 4-camera setup for a small home usually takes 1 day, while a large commercial warehouse may take a week or more. We will provide an exact timeline during the solution planning phase.
Do you provide AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts)?
Yes, we highly recommend our AMC services. They cover regular hardware health checks, priority support, and discounted repairs, ensuring your security system never experiences extended downtime.
Can I monitor my cameras remotely?
Absolutely. Almost all of our modern IP cameras and even newer analog DVRs support secure cloud integration. You can view live feeds and playback recordings directly from a smartphone app or a web browser anywhere in the world.
Do you support existing systems?
Yes. If you have an existing surveillance system that requires troubleshooting, repairs, or upgrading, our expert technicians can audit the setup and recommend the most cost-effective solution.
Do you provide rental services?
Yes, we offer CCTV rental services ideal for construction sites, temporary events, or short-term exhibitions. We handle the entire deployment, teardown, and maintenance during the rental period.
